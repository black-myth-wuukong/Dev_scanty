About

I like the kind of programming you can watch happen — a motor turning, a light blinking, a sensor reacting to something in the room. That's what pulled me toward robotics first, and it's the same instinct that got me into building for the web: seeing something respond immediately to what you just wrote.

Most of what's here is hands-on, self-directed learning — building small projects, breaking them, and figuring out why, one bug at a time.
